# Support

Need help with **LeadDirect-MCP**? We've got you covered! Whether you're setting up your first agentic outbound lead generation workflow or integrating advanced MCP capabilities, here's how you can get support.

## 📖 Documentation

The first place to look for help is our [README.md](README.md). It contains comprehensive guides on:
- Installation and Setup
- Connecting your AI (Claude Desktop, Cursor, etc.)
- Writing high-converting prompts for B2B lead generation
- Tool references

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

If you've encountered a bug or have a feature request to improve our agentic tools:
1. Check the [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/netizen4-bit/leaddirect-mcp/issues) to see if it has already been reported.
2. If not, open a new issue. Please provide as much detail as possible, including steps to reproduce the issue, your operating system, and the AI client you are using.

## 💬 Community Support

For general questions, discussions about outbound lead generation strategies, or sharing your success stories using LeadDirect-MCP:
- Start a discussion in the [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/netizen4-bit/leaddirect-mcp/discussions) tab.
- This is a great place to connect with other developers and sales professionals building agentic workflows.
